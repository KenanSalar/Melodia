# src/player/

The playback state machine and the DSP chain.

Each module's `//!` docs carry its own rationale, and the tuning constants are
justified at their definitions — read those first. This file holds only what
spans more than one module, or what breaks from outside the directory.

## Playback machine

- **Lock discipline is repo-wide — the rule is in the root `CLAUDE.md`.** The halves live here: `with_state_emit` (`state.rs`), `emit_and_execute` (`actions.rs`), ordering `exec_lock → PlayerState → rodio Player`. What it costs *this* directory is that the monitor decides under the `PlayerState` lock but acts after taking `exec_lock`, so a decision can go stale in the gap — which is why the crossfade builder re-verifies it (below).
- **Gapless playback** — Rodio queue 2-deep on the **active deck** via `preload_gapless()`; transition via polling `Player::len()` (single lock, avoid TOCTOU). Mutually exclusive with a crossfade *per transition*.
- **Position polling** — 500 ms `tokio::time::interval`; media position = `get_pos()` × `speed()`.
- **Speed/position live in two timelines.** rodio builds `source.speed(f).track_position()`, so `get_pos()` returns *output* time (`media / speed`) and `try_seek` is also output-time. Hence media position = `get_pos() × speed`; seeking a MEDIA position goes through `RodioPlayer::seek_to_media` = `try_seek(media / speed)`. **`set_speed` must re-anchor**: capture media position under the old speed, set the new speed, then `seek_to_media` — else the old-speed-accumulated time × the new speed rescales the elapsed portion and the slider jumps. rodio's `set_speed` is naive resampling (shifts pitch); pitch-preserved time-stretch is intentionally not done — hence the 2× cap.
- **Volume caps at unity.** `MAX_VOLUME = 100` (`state.rs`) is the true ceiling — no boost band above it. The single conversion to backend amplitude is `state::volume_to_amplitude(volume, is_muted)` (`volume/100`, `0.0` when muted); `effective_volume()` and the MPRIS path both route through it (don't re-roll `min(100)/100.0`). rodio's `set_volume` *does* accept `>1.0`, so lifting the ceiling later means adding clip protection, not just changing the constant.
- **Stale-playback safety in `execute_actions`.** `Path::exists()` pre-flight on every `PlayMedia`; decode errors handled identically: log, `rodio.stop()`, `queue.advance_skip()` via `with_state_emit`, prepend into draining `VecDeque<PlayerAction>`. Silent skip. `execute_actions` takes `&PlayerStateHandle` + `&PlayerSinks`.
- **A live source is the one thing here with no file behind it, and `PlayerState.source` is what says so.** `PlaybackSource::Station` means a station is on the deck and a track cannot also be, the two being one field. **Transport builders ask a capability rather than the variant** — `source_allows(PlaybackSource::advances_queue)` and its siblings — because each `is_radio()` branch was standing in for a different question, and a podcast answers them differently from both a file and a station. `is_radio` survives at five sites and every one of them is *about* a station: the pause that drops its socket, the play that cannot resume from one, the stop that forgets it, the session check, and the track that evicts it. A stream has no next, no previous, no seek, and speed is pinned to 1.0 (rodio implements speed by reporting a multiplied sample rate upward, which against a fixed-rate live source drifts the ring until it starves). It is the counterpart to the bullet above: there is no `Path` to pre-flight and a decode error is a reconnect rather than a skip, so `PlayerAction::PlayStream` is executed **outside `start_or_skip`** — nothing to auto-advance onto. **`radio_generation` is bumped by every transition that starts or ends a session**, so a connect that finishes after the user moved on is refused by the counter rather than by a flag; an open takes seconds and a click takes none. The queue underneath is left exactly as it was, which is what hands the library back on stop.
- **Symphonia** — one version, ours, reached through `decode`: the codec registry, the track pick and the packet loop, with `file_decode` and `stream_decode` differing only in what they open. Formats: MPEG audio (Layers I–III), FLAC, M4A/M4B (AAC, ALAC), Ogg Vorbis, WAV (PCM and ADPCM), AIFF/AIFF-C, Matroska, CAF. **The 0.6 feature list is the union of what stations serve and what `media::AUDIO_EXTENSIONS` ingests**, and `file_decode`'s fixture walk is what catches an entry going missing — a format the scanner offers and nothing plays looks like a broken file, not a manifest edit. It walks `AUDIO_EXTENSIONS` rather than naming fixtures, so a fourteenth extension owes a `silence.<ext>` beside its siblings; a list is precisely what cannot see the entry missing from it, and `.aac` — the raw ADTS case the whole move to 0.6 was for — is the one that went untested under one. **Gapless is narrower than the flag suggests**: `AudioDecoderOptions::gapless` defaults on, but in 0.6.1 only the MP3 and Vorbis decoders act on it, and `symphonia-format-isomp4` fills in neither the packet trims nor `Track::delay`/`padding`, so an iTunes `.m4a` keeps its encoder delay. Unchanged from 0.5, not lost in the move. `file_decode::probe_duration` builds the same decoder just to ask a file's length, is the scan path's only source of one for the containers lofty can't read, and is the sole edge `media/` has into this directory.
- **The sleep timer's end-of-track mode reaches in here.** `PlayerState::pause_after_current_track` is armed from `src/ui/sleep_timer.rs` and read by the monitor under its existing locks: the late-preload gate adds `&& !pause_after_current_track` so the track drains to `EndOfStream`, and `build_end_of_stream_actions` disarms + `stop_end_of_queue` instead of advancing (can't `player_pause` from inside that lock — non-reentrant). Arming within the final `PRELOAD_LEAD_MS` can't un-stage the preload, so it degrades to pausing at the *next* track's end.

## The DSP chain

Fixed order, innermost first — everything up to the tap lives inside one `EqSource`:

```
a file  → FileDecoder ────────┐
a station → StreamDecoder → ring → PrebufferSource ─┴→ EqSource:
    ReplayGain pre-gain → EQ bands → limiter → clamp → crossfade ramp → VisualizerTap
        → [rodio: speed → pause → volume] → mixer
```

- **The two heads are why `build_source` is generic over its `Source`, not over a reader**, and why there is no parallel `build_stream_source`. The *openings* stay two functions because they answer different questions — file open, extension hint, byte length and seekable, against mime type off the response and never seekable — while everything from the codec inward is `decode`'s and shared. Everything from `EqSource` rightward is shared, so EQ, the limiter and the visualizer work for a station for free; crossfade, gapless and ReplayGain are off, all three being per-track transitions or per-track tags a live stream has neither of.
- **The ring is there for the network, not for the decoder.** It used to be doing both: while local files decoded on rodio's Symphonia 0.5 and streams on our 0.6, `f32` crossing it was what kept any one file from naming two majors. That is over — `decode` answers for both — and what the ring still buys is the thing it was always really for, keeping a blocking socket read off the audio callback thread. A file needs none of that and gets none: `FileDecoder` is pulled inline, exactly as rodio's own decoder was.

- **Graphic equalizer = a custom biquad Rodio `Source` + lock-free shared state** (`equalizer.rs`; Rodio 0.22 has no peaking filters). `EqShared` (atomics + `generation`) drives `EqSource<S>` (one `biquad::DirectForm1` per band **per channel**, DF1 so live coefficient swaps don't transient), which `RodioPlayer` wraps around **both** `play_media` and `preload_gapless`; `next` rebuilds only on a `generation` change and **bypasses** (zero cost) when disabled + flat + 0 dB preamp. **Clip protection** (active-path-only): preamp → bands → a coupled soft-knee feed-forward peak limiter → clamp. **The limiter's attack/release are relative to the per-channel sample rate — feed `Source::sample_rate()` directly (already per-channel); do NOT divide by the channel count** (would run it ~N× too fast). EQ is **orthogonal to `PlayerState`** (infallible `library::playback::player_set_eq_*`, no `with_state_emit`); persists via `EqualizerFlags`; `Equalizer` global (`src/ui/equalizer.rs`) → `Dialog.kind == "equalizer"`. **`PRESETS` order is load-bearing** — the dropdown's inline `@tr` names + trailing "Custom" mirror it; the persisted sentinel `CUSTOM_PRESET` is absent from `PRESETS`.
- **ReplayGain is folded into the same `EqSource`** (`replaygain.rs`): a **per-track** linear pre-gain *before* the EQ bands (`out = x * preamp * rg_gain`), so the EQ's limiter guards a ReplayGain boost **for free**. The split is load-bearing: **per-track** values (`TrackReplayGain`) are **baked per source** at construction (the gapless-preloaded next track has different tags, so its gain rides its own `EqSource`); the **master** controls live in a lock-free `ReplayGainShared` (own `generation`). **⚠ RG is independent of the EQ toggle** — `rebuild` computes `rg_gain` *before* the EQ-off early return, and `next` polls **both** generations. **RG rides on `TrackSummary`** (4 `f32` cols) so the sync `play_track_inner` bakes it without an async fetch — threaded via `PlayMedia { replaygain }` and, for gapless, captured in `handlers.rs` from `queue.peek_next()` into `preload_gapless(path, baked_rg)` (**not** `PlayerAction::PreloadGapless`, which only *clears*). Orthogonal to `PlayerState`; persists via `ReplayGainFlags`. Preamp range symmetric ±15 (unlike the EQ's −12/+6).
- **`EqSource::next` polls all three generations (EQ/RG/fade) only at `frame_phase == 0`** — else a mid-frame `fade_ended` ends the source on a half frame and flips that deck's mixer channel parity permanently.

## Crossfade & decks

- **Two `Player` decks on one mixer + a ramp folded into `EqSource`** (`decks.rs`; rodio `Player` *sequences* sources, so overlap needs two `Player`s summed by the mixer). Each `Deck { player, fade: Arc<FadeShared> }` is `connect_new(mixer)` at boot with a `keep_alive_if_empty` queue. **Don't disable `keep_alive_if_empty`** — `false` *permanently detaches* a drained deck, the property the whole design rests on. **The ramp lives in `EqSource`, not `Player::set_volume`** (innermost → sample-accurate, counts **media** samples, lands after the `clamp(-1,1)`). **Curve is complementary linear** (`g_out + g_in ≡ 1`) — the mixer sums with **no clamping**, so post-clamp decks summing ≤ 1.0 is what stops clipping. `FadeShared` (`crossfade.rs`) is **deck-scoped** (exactly two); `EqSource::try_seek` **must not touch the fade fields**; the bypass path applies the ramp **per frame** (`frame_phase`) or a stereo frame shears. **Which deck a source lands on, and its ramp cell, are decided under the *same* lock acquisition as its `append`** (only `decode_file` is hoisted out) — `Decks::{cut_to, crossfade_to}` take a *builder* handed the target deck so the cell can only be the right one. `preload_gapless` snapshots `deck_epoch` before decode (every deck-replacing op bumps it) and **also refuses to stage behind an *empty* active deck** (`stop()`/deferred `ClearAll` empty without bumping the epoch, else a phantom `GaplessTransition`). `Player::clear()`/`try_seek` **block until the audio thread services them** (~5 ms) — a test pulling the mixer must run those on another thread.
- **The crossfade decision is two pure predicates, and the split is load-bearing** (`crossfade.rs`). `crossfade_eligible(...)` is **timing-independent**; `should_crossfade(...)` adds the timing + liveness terms and returns the fade length. Gate the monitor's late gapless preload on `!crossfade_eligible`, **not** `should_crossfade` — for a crossfade shorter than `PRELOAD_LEAD_MS` the latter would fire the preload first, set `gapless_pending`, and permanently block the crossfade via its own `!gapless_pending` gate. `should_crossfade` returns the **real remaining** media (never clamped up), so the ramp lands exactly on the track end. `same_album` requires `album.is_some()` — else two `None` albums compare equal and an untagged library never crossfades. **⚠ `MIN_CROSSFADE_MS = 1000` is bounded from below by the monitor's poll interval**: the trigger window is sampled once per `POLL_INTERVAL_MS` (500 ms), so a narrower window can be *stepped over* and degrade to a hard cut. Pinned by a `const` assert in `handlers.rs` (`MIN_CROSSFADE_MS >= MIN_FADE_MS + POLL_INTERVAL_MS`) + a sweep test. **A staged gapless source shares its deck's fade cell**, so `crossfade::manual_fade_ms` refuses to fade while `gapless_pending`, `play_media` re-checks it under the deck lock (the monitor's preload isn't under the `exec_lock`, so it can land mid-decision), and `begin_crossfade` `debug_assert!`s it.
- **`PlayerAction::BeginCrossfade` + `Pause { fade_ms }` + `Stop { fade_ms }`.** `build_crossfade_actions` mirrors `build_end_of_stream_actions` (`UpdatePlayCount` → `queue.advance()` → `begin_track` → `BeginCrossfade`); state advances at fade *start*, so Now-Playing switches as the overlap begins. **⚠ The decision is re-verified under the emit lock** — the monitor decides under the `PlayerState` lock but executes after taking `exec_lock`, where any control op can land. So the builder re-verifies the whole `crossfade::CrossfadeDecision`: bails unless `status == Playing` (guards a pause), `current_track.id == decision.track_id` (guards a pick/skip), and `position_ms == decision.position_ms` (the only tell for a `Seek`/restart). The three params are a struct so the two `u64`s can't be swapped. `StartMode::Crossfade` does **not** `rodio.stop()` on failure (the outgoing track is still audible). **Both `Pause` and `Stop` carry `fade_ms` from the caller** (`transport_fade_ms`). Everything the machine does for its own reasons passes `0` — including, **load-bearing**, the `Pause` that `build_next/previous_actions` append when paused, since they emit `[…, PlayMedia, Pause]` and `PlayMedia` *starts the deck* (a fade would ramp the incoming track down from full volume). Fade defers the real `pause()`/`clear()` on a `runtime.spawn` task guarded by `deck_epoch`, re-read **under the decks lock**; `seek()` deliberately does **not** bump it. **`can_fade_out` gate**: `fade_ms == 0` / `!active_deck_busy()` / `is_gapless_preloaded()` (staged source shares the deck's fade cell) / `is_crossfading()`. The deferred `ClearAll` drops the staged source **and** `gapless_pending` together (else a phantom `GaplessTransition`). Persists via `CrossfadeFlags`; **UI** is the one audio feature in **Settings → Playback** (four sub-rows under `if Settings.crossfade-enabled`, never `visible: false` — slint#7377).

## The live source and its reconnect

`stream_source.rs` and `prebuffer.rs` argue their own designs; what lives here is what crosses
modules. The half outside this directory — the facade, the off switch, the station tables — is
`.claude/rules/radio.md`.

- **The network may never touch the audio callback thread.** rodio pulls `Source::next()` inside the cpal data callback, so a blocking socket read stalls the whole mixer, local tracks included, for as long as the network is wedged. The ring decouples them: its feed thread fills, `PrebufferSource` pops without blocking, and a dry ring **yields silence rather than ending** — `Some(0.0)`, never `None`, since ending the source would tear the deck down on a stall the connection is about to recover from.
- **`current_span_len` must answer `Some`, and the bug it hides is only visible on the *second* station.** Answering `None` pins rodio's resampler to whatever rate the first station played at, for the rest of the process' life — every station after it plays fast or slow with nothing on screen to say why. The fault is in what the *mixer* built out of the answer, so no unit test on the source can see it; `tests/stream_rate.rs` plays two `PrebufferSource`s at different rates through one `Player` on a device-free mixer and reads the rate back off a square wave.
- **A partial frame is never split across the ring and silence.** The pop is whole-frame gated, or a stereo frame shears at the starvation boundary and that deck's mixer channel parity flips permanently — the same hazard `EqSource::next`'s `frame_phase == 0` gate exists for.
- **Reconnect lives in the feed thread, not in the monitor.** That thread already holds the URL, the client and the ring, so it re-opens and keeps filling the *same* ring: the rodio source never ends, the deck never blinks, and the state machine needs no reconnect path at all. In the monitor it would mean handing `handlers.rs` an HTTP client and inverting the `library` → `player` dependency direction. The monitor's end-of-stream arm only ever sees a station that has already spent its budget, and it **stops rather than advancing the queue** — a station going off air must not start playing a library track.
- **`Loading` means the initial connect and nothing else.** Every gap after the first audio — starvation or reconnect — stays `Playing` with `radio.buffering` raised, which is what keeps a rebuffer from flipping MPRIS to Stopped or clearing Discord presence. Neither reporting site needed an arm for it, and that is the point. `buffering` lives on `RadioNowPlaying` rather than on `PlayerState` because it means nothing without a station.
- **Pausing a station drops its connection.** `stream-download`'s bounded storage back-pressures the server when the reader falls behind, so a held-open socket resumes on stale audio. Pause keeps the station on screen with a play button that re-opens it; `library::playback::needs_station_reopen` is the pure predicate routing that, shared with `player_play` so the two can't disagree.

## Visualizer — audio side

`visualizer.rs` and `spectrum.rs` argue the design in their `//!` docs and justify
every tuning constant at its definition; `waveform.rs` does the same for the trace.
The UI half — arming, window-visibility gating, styles — is `.claude/rules/visualizer.md`,
which spans `src/ui/visualizer/` and the strip's `.slint` under `melodia-ui/ui/`.
What lives here is what crosses modules:

- **The tap is read-only and bit-identical.** `VisualizerTap<S>` wraps `EqSource` — after the bands, ReplayGain, the limiter's clamp and the crossfade ramp, but *before* rodio's speed/pause/volume wrappers, so the volume slider can't flatten the bars. Wrapping rather than living inside `EqSource` is what keeps that module's `frame_phase == 0` poll gate and bypass path clear of a change with nothing to do with them. (Clementine's per-pipeline probe and Audacious's post-effect, pre-volume vis buffer tap at the same point.)
- **One ring per deck; `snapshot` sums the live ones.** A crossfade has two taps writing at once, and rodio's `MixerSource` pulls one sample from *every* live voice per output sample — a shared ring would interleave the two tracks rather than sum them (each at half rate plus a square wave at Nyquist, for the whole overlap). Summed, the window *is* the mixer's output: each tap sees its own deck's already-**ramped** signal, and the ramp is complementary linear over sources that have already clamped, so the sum stays inside ±1. MPD mixes its two songs' PCM the same way.
- **Which rings are live is drop-scoped, not a heuristic** — a tap holds a `DeckRun` claiming its deck for the life of the source. A cursor-delta "did it advance since last tick?" would read a live deck as dead, since CPAL pulls a whole period at a time. Opening a run on an **idle** deck also stamps `valid_from`, so an incoming crossfade can't mix in the tail of what that deck played two tracks ago; a gapless successor staged behind a still-live source keeps the history, which is correct — that audio is continuous.
- **Known cosmetic race, documented rather than bought:** `clear()` returns on the queue's end-of-source signal, which rodio sends a couple of instructions before it replaces (and drops) the source, so a hard cut can find the outgoing claim still standing and skip its history drop. Self-heals as the window refills, and is exactly what one shared ring did all the time. Both deterministic fixes cost real structure — a fresh-vs-continuing flag threaded through every `build_source` call site, or `Deck` holding the shared cell (a `decks` ↔ `visualizer` cycle that still needs a hand-added stamp in `crossfade_to`).
- **Analyse against `analysis_rate()` (rate × speed), never `sample_rate()`.** rodio's `Speed` forwards samples verbatim and implements speed purely by reporting a multiplied `sample_rate()` upward, so analysing the ring against the media rate plots the *file's* pitch: at 2× a 1 kHz tone is heard an octave up while the 1 kHz bar lights. `RodioPlayer::set_speed` is that cell's only writer — boot hydration goes through it, and `play_media`/`begin_crossfade` only re-apply what it published.
- **Mixed sample *rates* across an overlap stay approximate.** One `sample_rate` cell is analysed against (the incoming track's, published by its tap on its first frame), so the outgoing track's contribution is time-scaled by the rate ratio for the length of the fade — under a semitone, on a decoration.
- **`RING_CAP` is sized for the *waveform's* span** at the highest rate a file plausibly carries — *not* 2× `FFT_SIZE`, which would silently narrow the trace. The trace's span is a fixed number of **milliseconds** resolved against `analysis_rate()`.
- **`spectrum::NUM_BANDS` is the sole bar count.** Slint iterates the model (`for level in Visualizer.bars`), so changing it needs no `.slint` edit — but `src/ui/visualizer/mod.rs` must keep its two uses (the `VecModel` sizing and `SpectrumAnalyzer::new`) in lockstep or the per-band `set_row_data` loop silently truncates.
- **Retuning is measured, and the number to read is p90 per band** — a mean is dragged to zero by every track with nothing in that band, where p90 asks how tall the bar gets when there *is* something to show. The harness is a throwaway: decode a couple of dozen real tracks through the actual `SpectrumAnalyzer` at the real tick, reporting per-band mean / p50 / p90 / pinned% / dead%. `TILT_DB_PER_OCTAVE` reads low against CAVA's 5.1 because RSS already tilts broadband ~3 dB/octave for free — the *total* is what has to land near 5, and double-counting inverts the fault into a dead bass end.
